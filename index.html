Research Group

We design ICs that adapt power delivery, sense with precision, and keep running at the edge, and on or inside the body.

Integrated Power & Sensing Systems (IPSS) is a research group at Tampere University, led by Dr. Abrar Akram. We co-design adaptive power delivery, sensing interfaces, and mixed-signal integrated systems — from circuit to system level — to build energy-efficient IC platforms for next-generation edge and bio-integrated applications.

Research Group

We design mixed-signal ICs that adapt power delivery, sense with precision, and keep running at the edge, and on or inside the body.

Integrated Power & Sensing Systems (IPSS) is a research group at Tampere University, led by Dr. Abrar Akram, building energy-efficient mixed-signal IC platforms for next-generation edge and bio-integrated applications — from circuit to system level.

Openings

Join the group

We're always interested in hearing from motivated students and researchers. Here's what we look for and how to apply.

PhD Students

Openings are typically tied to [department]'s admissions cycle. Email the PI with your CV and a short note on your interests before applying.

Master's / Undergrad

We host thesis and research-assistant projects each semester. Get in touch to discuss current openings.

Postdocs

Open to postdoc applications, including fellowship co-applications. Contact the PI directly.

Process

How to apply

01
Read a couple of our recent papers on the publications page to make sure your interests align with ours.
02
Email the PI with your CV, a short paragraph on why you're interested, and (for PhD applicants) unofficial transcripts.
03
If it's a good fit, we'll set up a call to talk about ongoing projects and expectations.

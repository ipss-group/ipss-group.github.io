What we work on

Research

We design mixed-signal ICs that sense precisely, manage power intelligently, and keep running — at the edge, and on or inside the body.

Theme 01

Power That Anticipates

Predictive Power Delivery & Management

Heterogeneous computing platforms run workloads whose power demands shift constantly and unpredictably. We design predictive power delivery systems that anticipate these shifts in real time, dynamically matching supply to load. The result: higher efficiency and reliability without the overhead of static, worst-case provisioning. This work spans circuit-level converter design up through system-level control, closing the loop between prediction and delivery.

Theme 02

Sensing at the Source

Integrated Sensing Interfaces

Every intelligent system starts with a signal worth trusting. We design integrated sensor interface ICs — including high-precision current readout circuits — that capture real-world signals accurately, at low power and minimal area. This front-end layer is what lets edge and bio-integrated devices perceive their environment reliably, without relying on bulky or power-hungry external components.

sensing interfacescurrent readoutedge sensing
Theme 03

Powering What's Inside

Wireless & Self-Powered Bioelectronics

Devices placed on or inside the body can't rely on batteries or bulky wired connections. We design self-powered and wireless-powered circuits for implantable microsystems — from energy harvesting front-ends to efficient wireless power receivers — enabling long-term operation with minimal size, heat, and maintenance. This work targets the specific constraints of bio-integrated deployment: safety, miniaturization, and reliability over years, not just efficiency on paper.
